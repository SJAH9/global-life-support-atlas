# Method specification: development 0.1

## Research question

Does a nested model of regional life-support resilience predict independently specified harm and adaptation outcomes better than carbon metrics alone?

The question is comparative. The project does not assume that the life-support model will outperform a carbon baseline.

## Enclosure

```text
E(life-support conditions) [ D(observed departure) | E(harm, response, adaptation) ]
```

The outer enclosure contains water, food, heat, soil, energy, transport, health, ecological integrity, and institutional capacity. Carbon remains a measured condition nested in energy and production, with joins to the other eight domains. Outcomes and evaluation metrics must be specified independently of the predictors used to build the score.

## Initial aggregation

1. Retain every source value in its native unit.
2. Require a published direction and reference interval before normalization.
3. Clamp normalized indicator values to `[0, 1]` while retaining source values.
4. Average accepted indicators within a domain.
5. Publish no composite until all nine domains are represented.
6. Combine the nine domain values by geometric mean.

The geometric mean makes low-domain resilience consequential and limits compensation by unrelated abundance. This is an initial testable rule, not a permanent fact. Every release must publish sensitivity to weights, bounds, missingness, lag, geography, and alternative aggregation.

## Evidence states

- `official`: supplied as an official value by the named producer.
- `modeled`: generated by a disclosed model.
- `estimated`: estimated by a producer or project method.
- `imputed`: filled under a disclosed imputation rule.
- `community-submitted`: deposited but not accepted as reviewed evidence.
- `reviewed`: passed project review for the stated use.

Evidence state does not function as a truth flag. It preserves how the observation entered the enclosure.
