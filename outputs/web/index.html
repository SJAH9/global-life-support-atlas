The reduction of a life-support continuum to a politically manageable carbon frame.

Carbon remains measurable and materially important. It sits inside energy, production, ecology, food, water, heat, transport, and health rather than replacing them.

The atlas tests whether nested life-support conditions predict observed harm and adaptive capacity better than carbon measures alone.
