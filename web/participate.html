Public questions

Open a correctly enclosed question about harm, adaptation, or an omitted life-support relationship.

Review boundary

A submission enters a review state. It must retain its author, source, time, place, unit, method, license, and uncertainty. Approval makes it eligible for the evidence field; it does not guarantee inclusion in a score. Rejected and superseded records remain auditable where rights permit.
